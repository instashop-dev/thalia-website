Aussie Living Lifestyle Products is an Australian electronics retailer selling through its Shopify store at solarfountain.com.au, run by owner Jon. Like other merchants running Amazon FBA and Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) alongside Shopify, the business faced the dual-channel fulfillment challenge common to sellers on both platforms — and has now run on Shipr for more than two years, longer than any other Shipr merchant in this case study series.

Before Shipr, Jon fulfilled every Shopify order that needed Amazon FBA handling manually. The core problems this created were slow operations and a growing staff workload, both of which get worse as order volume increases rather than better — a manual fulfillment process doesn't scale gracefully.

Jon adopted Shipr specifically to automate Amazon FBA fulfillment and use Amazon's Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) capability. Automatic Order Fulfillment became the single most-used feature, routing Shopify orders to Amazon FBA/MCF without manual intervention for each order.

Shipr eliminated 75–90% of Aussie Living Lifestyle Products' manual fulfillment work, and the team now saves 10–20 hours every week. Jon rates his fulfillment process a 4 out of 5 since adopting Shipr and describes the app's reliability as "Extremely Reliable" — the top tier available, and a notable distinction after two-plus years of continuous use. Asked what would have happened without Shipr, he pointed to more manual work, more fulfillment errors, and difficult scaling. His own summary of the app's value: "Automatically syncing orders to MCF."
