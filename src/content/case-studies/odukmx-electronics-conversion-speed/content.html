Oduk.mx is the second of Mauricio Fajardo's three Shopify stores, focused on electronics and health products in Mexico. Where a sister store is about building catalogue breadth quickly, Oduk.mx's use of Robo highlights a different angle on the same tool: using fast imports as a direct sales conversion aid, not just a back-office efficiency gain.

Before Robo, each product import at Oduk.mx took 5–10 minutes using manual copy-paste from supplier websites. Beyond the drag on catalogue growth, Mauricio needed a way to save time importing, avoid manual copy-paste, migrate products between stores, and build the catalogue faster — a broader set of needs than speed alone.

Robo's Chrome Extension and Product URL Import, used together, let Mauricio pull in product titles, descriptions, images, and tags in a single fast action. Image Import and One-Click Import stood out as favorite features, reflecting how central visual product data is to a fast-moving electronics and health catalogue.

Import time dropped from 5–10 minutes to just 1–2 minutes per product, a reduction of more than 90%. Oduk.mx's catalogue grew by an estimated 10–25%, with faster product launches and a better overall workflow. Mauricio rated Robo a perfect 5 out of 5 for likelihood to recommend, and describes using Robo's speed to import and show a product to a customer in the moment: "It helps me generate sales of related products and show the product to my client in 2 minutes, seriously impacting conversion and ease of purchase for my customers."
