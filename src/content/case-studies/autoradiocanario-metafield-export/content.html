Autoradiocanario.com is an electronics retailer on Shopify managing a catalogue of 500–1,000 products, run by owner Paolo Longo. Unlike merchants whose needs center on pricing or product import, Paolo's challenge was narrower and more technical: getting Shopify's product metafield data out of the platform reliably, in order to feed a custom application his business depends on.

Before Product Data Exporter Pro, Paolo relied on Shopify's native CSV export. The specific gap he ran into was that this standard export was missing important fields — namely, product metafields, the custom data attached to products beyond Shopify's default fields. For a business feeding that data into a custom app, an export that leaves out metafields isn't a partial solution, it's effectively no solution for that integration.

Product Data Exporter Pro's Metafields Export capability gave Paolo the specific, missing piece: the ability to export metafield data alongside standard product data, directly from Shopify. Paolo now uses this feature weekly to keep his custom app integration fed with accurate, complete product data.

Paolo describes the impact plainly: the app "resolved all my problem." Asked why he chose Product Data Exporter Pro over other solutions, he pointed to ease of use, metafield export capability, and that the app is built for Shopify specifically. Asked to describe the app in one sentence, his answer was blunt and positive: "cheap and easy." He confirmed he would recommend the app to other merchants.
