  - question: 'How do I add an external link button to a Shopify product page?'
    answer: 'Add a destination URL to the product, choose clear CTA text, and display the button in the product-page template. For larger catalogs, use a link-management workflow or app so destinations can be mapped and audited without editing theme code product by product.'

    answer: 'Yes, when the affiliate program allows your promotion method and you disclose the relationship clearly. Keep the affiliate ID intact, use honest button copy, and verify the destination for each product or variant.'
  - question: 'How do I track external link clicks in GA4?'
    answer: 'Use consistent UTM parameters on campaign links and configure outbound-click events where the interaction happens. GA4 can report source, medium, campaign, and content values, while affiliate-network conversions may require a separate partner dashboard.'
