  - question: Can Amazon Fulfill Orders From My Shopify Store?
    answer: Yes, Amazon MCF can fulfill eligible Shopify orders using inventory stored in Amazon fulfillment centers. You need a supported integration and an account, inventory, and destination setup that meet the service requirements. Check availability for your market.
  - question: What Is the Difference Between Amazon FBA and MCF?
    answer: FBA fulfills Amazon marketplace orders, while MCF fulfills orders from other sales channels. They can use a shared inventory pool, but their fulfillment services and fees differ. A Shopify order is an off-Amazon order.
  - question: Do I Need Matching SKUs on Shopify and Amazon?
    answer: You need an accurate correspondence between each Shopify variant and its Amazon inventory SKU. Matching identifiers simplify this, while supported SKU mapping can handle differences. Check pack sizes and variants before enabling automatic fulfillment.
  - question: Does an Amazon Integration Automatically Sync Inventory?
    answer: Inventory syncing depends on the app, plan, and configuration. Confirm whether your setup offers manual, scheduled, or continuous updates. Test the result before relying on it for stock availability.
