# Security Update: PHP Vulnerabilities Resolved

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

We recently addressed two reported PHP security vulnerabilities, **CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619**, also referred to as PHP2Shell.

Our technical team applied the necessary security patches and upgraded the PHP version used by our application from **PHP 8.3.22 to PHP 8.3.36**.

## What security measures were taken?

| Completed action | What it means |
|---|---|
| **PHP version upgraded** | PHP was updated from 8.3.22 to 8.3.36. |
| **Security fixes applied** | The required patches were installed. |
| **Application verified** | We confirmed the fixes are active and the application loads and operates normally. |
| **Security checks completed** | Available server logs were reviewed for attempted exploitation or suspicious activity. No such signs were identified during our investigation. |

## Does this affect your store or data?

**No action is required from you.**

The security update has been completed on our end, and the application is running normally. The update did not change your existing data, login credentials, or application settings.

You can continue using the application as usual.

## Our commitment to security

We take the security of our application and our customers’ data seriously. We continue to monitor our infrastructure, review potential security risks, and apply necessary updates to help maintain a secure and reliable environment.

If you have questions or concerns about this security update, please [contact our support team](/contact). We will be happy to assist you.

Thank you for your continued trust in our application.

## FAQ

**Q: What vulnerabilities were addressed?**

We addressed CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, also referred to as PHP2Shell.

**Q: What PHP version is now being used?**

The affected application environment was upgraded from PHP 8.3.22 to PHP 8.3.36.

**Q: Do merchants need to do anything?**

No. The update was completed by our technical team. You do not need to reinstall the application or change your store settings.

**Q: Was merchant data accessed or leaked?**

Based on our review of the available server logs, we found no evidence of attempted exploitation, suspicious activity, or merchant-data access or leakage connected to this issue.
